AstroAk has ended its services

Dear friends,

I built AstroAk with a great deal of care, to calculate the sky correctly and to tell the history of astrology.

Over time I saw something. Most people who came here were not looking for a calculation, but for an answer. They wanted to learn their future and make their decisions by it. The personal readings I published on the site opened the door to this as well. I added warnings, I set limits, but it was not enough.

Our calculations were correct. But someone who reads this information the wrong way can lead both themselves and the people they love in the wrong direction. My heart could not agree to be the means of that.

Because I hold your peace of mind and your being rightly guided above everything else, I have decided to close AstroAk.

Thank you, from the bottom of my heart, for the interest and trust you have shown until today.

Finally, I want to tell you this sincerely: The sky at the moment you were born does not decide who you are or what you will live through. Even twins born at the same hour, in the same place, become different people and live different lives. Astrology cannot draw your character, cannot write your fate, cannot know your future. Do not give money or anything else to anyone who says they know these things or can change them. Always make your important decisions by consulting your conscience and your heart.

With love and respect,

Raşit

Founder, AstroAk
