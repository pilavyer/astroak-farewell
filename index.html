AstroAk has ended its services

Dear friends,

I built AstroAk with a great deal of effort and good intentions, to calculate the sky correctly and, by interpreting it correctly, to be of real use to people. Thanks to your goodwill, in a very short time we earned an important place among astrology sites. This made me very happy, because I believed that through astrology I was helping people get to know and understand themselves. That is also why everything on the site was unlimited, free and without ads.

But over time I saw something else. Most of the people who came here were not trying to know themselves; they wanted news of the future. They wanted to pass judgment in advance on themselves and on others, to decide beforehand whether they would get along with someone they had never met. Couples about to marry or divorce were searching a chart for the answer to their shared future. People struggling with illness or deep emotional distress were trying to learn the date on which their troubles would end. Some put off a new job, a move, even a medical treatment until a “favorable transit” came along. Others drew away from someone they loved simply because their charts looked incompatible.

This was something I never approved of. As someone who knows astrology well, and as the founder of AstroAk, I said again and again in my writing that it should not be used this way. I added warnings, I set limits. But it was not enough. I saw with my own eyes how thousands of people were using the site. For most of them, AstroAk had stopped being a tool for something useful and had become a tool for fortune-telling.

What is more, I saw that the benefit called “getting to know yourself” is very often an illusion. Someone draws up their birth chart and says, “This is who I am. My Mars is in this house, that is why I act this way.” Then hospital records show that their birth time was wrong. The chart they had recognized themselves in for years was in fact a different chart, and yet it still seemed to them to “fit”. Here astrology contradicts itself. Worse still, many people use this knowledge to shoot themselves in the foot: they put off their decisions, end their relationships, and lock themselves and others inside a chart.

Many astrologers know this too. That is why they begin with a warning: “Astrology decides nothing; it only shows a potential.” Then they talk for hours about Mars transits, Saturn cycles and past lives. If this knowledge should not be put at the center of life, why spend two hours explaining a single Mars transit? Because, very often, it is a very good source of income.

I could have chosen this path as well. By building up my profession and AstroAk's name, I could have earned a great deal of money and kept your mind busy with Mercury and Saturn cycles without telling you the truth. I did not do that; I withdrew my entire investment. Because I built AstroAk not to make money but to be useful to people, and I saw that it was not.

Since Hippocrates, the first precept of medicine has been this: First, do no harm. I could not keep running something that did good on one side and harm on the other, not even with good intentions. Because I hold your peace of mind and your being rightly guided above everything else, I have decided to close AstroAk.

Thank you, from the bottom of my heart, for the interest and trust you have shown until today.

Finally, I want to tell you this sincerely: The sky at the moment you were born does not decide who you are or what you will live through. Even twins born at the same hour, in the same place, become different people and live different lives. Astrology cannot draw your character, cannot write your fate, cannot know your future. Do not give money or anything else to anyone who says they know these things or can change them. Always make your important decisions by consulting your conscience and your heart.

With love and respect,

Raşit

Founder, AstroAk
